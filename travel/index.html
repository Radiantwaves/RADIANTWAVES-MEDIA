Specialist guidance

HND holders: understand your progression options

An HND is assessed differently by universities and countries. Direct Master's entry cannot be assumed. We research possible routes based on the applicant's actual academic record and the institution's written requirements.

  • Assessment of HND subject, institution and grade.
  • Review of transcript, work experience and additional qualifications.
  • Research into Bachelor's top-up, advanced entry and bridging routes.
  • Check whether a PGD or other qualification could support later Master's entry.
  • Written pathway report with university links and next steps.
We do not promise admission or claim that every European university accepts an HND. The receiving institution makes the final decision.
